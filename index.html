Help Center

Quick answers for planning a rental.

What do I need to rent?

A valid driver's license and a credit card in the driver's name. Drivers must be 21 or older.

Is there a mileage limit?

Daily rentals include 200 miles. Weekly long-drive plans include unlimited mileage.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Later changes may keep one day's rate.

Do you offer airport pickup?

Yes. Meet an Anti agent at major airports in every city listed in the search bar.
